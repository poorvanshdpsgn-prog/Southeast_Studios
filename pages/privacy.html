Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

This notice describes how Southeast Studios handles information when you use BotForge accounts and tools. BotForge projects are stored locally in your browser in the current version; they are not synced to our project servers.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

“Southeast Studios” refers to the team operating BotForge. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, email studiossoutheast@gmail.com. Please do not send your password or other unnecessary sensitive information in your message.

2. Information involved

  • Account information: your email address and display name, used for account creation, sign-in, and showing your signed-in identity.
  • Authentication information: passwords are submitted directly to Firebase Authentication for account verification. BotForge does not receive or store your password in its own project storage. Firebase may process account and technical data under Google’s service terms and privacy documentation.
  • Browser-local BotForge data: projects, scenes, game logic, imported assets, preferences, and certain local activity are saved in your browser using IndexedDB or local storage. This data remains on your device in the current version unless you export it or otherwise share it yourself.
  • Support information: information you choose to include when you email us, which we use to respond to your request.

3. Why information is used

We use account information to create and authenticate accounts, protect the sign-in flow, display your account identity, respond to support requests, maintain the service, and meet legal obligations. Browser-local data is used by your browser to save and reopen your BotForge work and preferences.

4. Service providers and sharing

Firebase Authentication, operated by Google, provides sign-in and account authentication. Using the sign-in feature sends information needed for authentication to Firebase. Google’s handling of information is also described in the Firebase privacy and security documentation and Google Privacy Policy.

We do not currently sync BotForge project contents or imported assets to a Southeast Studios cloud project service. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

5. Storage and retention

Firebase retains account information according to its service configuration and applicable provider terms. Browser-local projects and preferences remain in your browser until you delete them, clear browser storage, or remove the relevant site data. Exported JSON files are outside our control once saved to your device. Support emails are retained as needed to respond and manage the request or meet legal obligations.

You can request assistance with your account or personal information by emailing us. We will review and respond as required by applicable law. Deleting browser data may erase local projects, so export anything you want to keep first.

6. Your choices and requests

You may choose not to create an account, and you can stop using BotForge at any time. You can edit or delete projects using the editor, export projects, or clear local browser storage through your browser settings. For requests about account information, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or a privacy complaint, contact studiossoutheast@gmail.com. We may need to verify a request before acting and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

7. Children and young users

BotForge may be used by young creators. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must review and accept the Terms on your behalf and supervise your use. The current signup flow does not independently verify a parent’s identity or parental consent, and the signup checkbox is not a verification mechanism. A parent or guardian who has a concern or wishes to make a request regarding a child’s information can email us. Where applicable law requires verifiable parental consent or additional safeguards, those requirements apply; we will update the account flow and this notice as needed.

8. Security

We use Firebase Authentication for sign-in rather than handling passwords ourselves. No internet service or local browser storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a unique password, protect access to your device, and contact us promptly if you suspect account misuse.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Policy as BotForge changes or legal requirements take effect. We will revise the effective date above and give notice of material changes where appropriate.

10. Contact

Privacy questions, requests, or complaints: studiossoutheast@gmail.com.
